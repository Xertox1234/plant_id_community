## What we collect and why

**Your account.** Your email, username, first and last name, bio, location
(free text, if you enter one), website and avatar. If you sign in with Google,
we record that sign-in. On the app, your login uses Google Firebase, so we
store your Firebase user ID. When you sign up you confirm that you are 14 or
older and accept this policy and the Terms of Service; we record which version
you accepted and when. We use this to run your account and show your profile.

Your username and avatar are public. Your first and last name are shown with a
blog article you write or an event you organise. We do not show your email
address to other users. We remove the avatar photo's location and camera data
(EXIF) before storing it.

**Plant scans.** The photos you take to identify a plant or check its health.
They are stored privately, and we re-encode them to remove EXIF data. We send
the photos to our plant identification providers to get a result.

**Garden data.** The beds, plants, care tasks and harvests you record, and the
plants you save in the app (stored in Google Firebase). We use this to run your
garden tracking.

**Plant health checks.** When you ask for a health check, you can type a note
about where the plant is (for example "east-facing window"). It is free text
you choose to enter, up to 200 characters. We store it with your health check
request, and only you can see it. We use it to keep your diagnosis history.

**Community events.** If you create an event, you can enter its venue, street
address, map coordinates (latitude and longitude), and a contact email and
phone number. These are details you choose to type in; we do not read them from
your device. You choose who can see the event (anyone, people in your area or
hardiness zone, people you follow, or only you). Everyone who can see the event
sees what you entered, including the address and contact details, and for a
public event that includes visitors who are not signed in. We use them to list
the event and let people find and contact you about it.

**Forum.** Your posts, topics, private messages, the reports you make, and the
people you block or mute. We use this to run the forum and to moderate it.

**Automated spam screening.** Software checks forum posts and private messages
for spam before they are delivered. For members who are new or not yet trusted,
this check can send the text to OpenAI. A post that fails the check waits for a
moderator. A private message that fails the check is not sent and is not kept.
If you think that was a mistake, email [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] and a person will
review what happened.

**Newsletter.** Your subscription, if you sign up.

**Push notifications.** A device token from your phone (Firebase Cloud
Messaging or Apple Push Notification service) or your web browser, so we can
send you notifications you turn on.

**Email records.** When we send you email, we record whether it was delivered
or bounced, and which links you clicked. If an address must no longer receive
email, we keep a hashed marker of it so we do not email it again. We use these
records to deliver email, and to know which links in our emails are used.

**Blog page views.** When you view a blog page, we record your IP address,
browser (user agent) and the page you came from (referrer). We use this for site
statistics and to prevent abuse.

**Rate limiting.** We hold IP addresses briefly in memory to stop abuse, such
as too many requests.

**Audit log.** A record of changes to your account and profile (never your
password), your plant identifications and collections, and your garden data. We
use it for security, and to investigate problems.

**Error reports.** When the website or our servers hit an error, an error
report is sent to Sentry. On the website, a session that hits an error is
recorded, with text masked. Sessions without an error are not recorded. The
app sends no error reports. We use these to find and fix errors.

**Purchases.** If you buy Premium in the app, we receive your purchase status
from Apple or Google, based on the store receipt. In-app purchase is being
built before launch.

**Cookies and browser storage.** The website sets cookies that keep you signed
in and protect forms against forgery. It also keeps a few things in your
browser's storage, such as your display settings, a copy of your profile and
forum drafts you have not sent. We set no advertising cookies. A YouTube or
Vimeo video embedded in a forum post may set its own cookies when you play it.

We do not collect your device's location. Any place or address in this policy
is something you type in yourself.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, such as health, financial or
religious details. Please do not post it.

**What we do not do.** We show no ads. We use no advertising or third-party
analytics trackers. We do record which links in our emails are clicked and
which blog pages are viewed, as described above. We do not sell your data.

## Who receives it

We use these service providers to run Houseplant MD. Each receives only what it
needs for its task, and acts for us:

- **Plant.id (FlowerChecker s.r.o., Czech Republic) and PlantNet (France):**
  your scan photos, to identify plants and check their health.
- **Google Firebase (United States):** login, saved plants and push
  notifications.
- **Apple (United States):** push notifications.
- **OpenAI (United States):** forum posts and private messages from new or
  not-yet-trusted members, to screen them for spam, while that screen is
  switched on; and the forum text you submit to an optional AI feature
  (search, writing help, plant-care answers) when you use one.
- **Sentry (United States):** error reports, and the masked recording of a
  website session that hit an error.
- **Cloudflare (United States):** the website and file storage.
- **Railway (United States):** our servers and database.
- **Resend (United States):** sending email.
- **Apple and Google (United States):** in-app purchase status from store
  receipts.

YouTube and Vimeo are not our service providers. When you play a video that a
user embedded in a forum post, it loads from that service, which then collects
information under its own policy.

## How long it is kept

- **Account, garden data, saved plants, forum content, push tokens and
  newsletter subscription:** until you remove them or delete your account.
- **Scans:** a scan you have not decided to keep is deleted after 24 hours. A
  scan you keep stays until you remove it or delete your account.
- **Email delivery and bounce records:** 90 days.
- **Email click records:** 90 days.
- **Hashed "do not email" marker:** kept indefinitely.
- **Blog page views:** 90 days.
- **Rate-limiting IP addresses:** briefly, in memory only.
- **Audit log:** 90 days.

### When you delete your account

Deletion is immediate. We email you when it is done.

**What is erased:** your account, profile and avatar; your garden data; your
scans and their photos; your saved plants and your Firebase login; your push
tokens; your newsletter subscription; your block and mute lists; and your
address in our email log. Your forum posts are erased: the text, images and
edit history are removed, and a "[deleted]" placeholder keeps each post's place
so threads still read.

Audit log entries about your account are removed within 90 days by our regular
clean-up.

**What is kept:**

- A record that the account existed and was deleted, with a hashed copy of the
  email address. If you deleted the account yourself, the hash is removed
  after 90 days, and it never stops you from signing up again. If staff deleted
  the account for abuse, or because its owner was under 14, the hash is kept
  and blocks that address from signing up again.
- The hashed "do not email" marker.
- Moderation records.
- Posts under an open abuse report, until the report is closed.
- Text of yours that other people quoted, and mentions of you, in their posts.
- The other person's copy of your private messages, shown as from "[deleted]".

When staff delete an account because its owner was under 14, we go further:
posts under a report are erased at once, the messages that person sent are
deleted from the other person's copy, their words are removed from quotes in
other posts, and the text of reports they filed is erased.

## Your rights

You can ask to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it. You can delete your account yourself in the app's settings, in
  the website's settings, or at [/delete-account](/delete-account). You can
  edit your own forum posts, and you can ask us to remove any of them;
- **receive a copy** of it in a portable format;
- **withdraw your consent**, or object to a use of your information.

Email [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We answer within 30 days, or sooner where the law
where you live sets a shorter time. Asking is free.

You can also complain to a privacy regulator:

- in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC);
- in Alberta, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of
  Alberta (OIPC);
- in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI);
- in Mexico, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.
