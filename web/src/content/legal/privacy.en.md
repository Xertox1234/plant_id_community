## What we collect and why

**Your account.** Your email, username, first and last name, bio, location
(free text, if you enter one), website and avatar. If you sign in with Google
or GitHub, we record that sign-in. On the app, your login uses Google Firebase,
so we store your Firebase user ID. When you sign up you confirm that you are 13
or older and accept this policy and the Terms of Service; we record which
version you accepted and when. We use this to run your account and show your
profile.

Your avatar is public. We remove the photo's location and camera data (EXIF)
before storing it.

**Plant scans.** The photos you take to identify a plant or check its health.
They are stored privately, and we re-encode them to remove EXIF data. We send
the photos to our plant identification providers to get a result.

**Garden data.** The beds, plants, care tasks and harvests you record, and the
plants you save in the app (stored in Google Firebase). We use this to run your
garden tracking.

**Forum.** Your posts, topics, private messages, the reports you make, and the
people you block or mute. We use this to run the forum and to moderate it.

**Newsletter.** Your subscription, if you sign up.

**Push notifications.** A device token from your phone (Firebase Cloud
Messaging or Apple Push Notification service) or your web browser, so we can
send you notifications you turn on.

**Email records.** When we send you email, we record whether it was delivered
or bounced, and which links you clicked. If an address must no longer receive
email, we keep a hashed marker of it so we do not email it again. We use these
records to deliver email, and to know which links in our emails are used.

**Blog page views.** When you view a blog page, we record your IP address,
browser (user agent) and the page you came from (referrer). We use this for site
statistics and to prevent abuse.

**Rate limiting.** We hold IP addresses briefly in memory to stop abuse, such
as too many requests.

**Audit log.** A record of changes to your account and profile (never your
password), your plant identifications and collections, and your garden data. We
use it for security, and to investigate problems.

**Crash reports.** When the app or the website hits an error, a crash report
is sent to Sentry. On the website, about 10% of sessions, and every session with
an error, are recorded, with text masked. We use these to find and fix errors.

**Purchases.** If you buy Premium in the app, we receive your purchase status
from Apple or Google, based on the store receipt. In-app purchase is being
built before launch.

**What we do not do.** We show no ads. We use no analytics or tracking SDKs. We
do not sell your data.

## Who receives it

We use these service providers to run Houseplant MD. Each receives only what it
needs for its task:

- **Plant.id (Kindwise) and PlantNet:** your scan photos, to identify plants
  and check their health.
- **Google Firebase:** login, saved plants and push notifications.
- **Apple:** push notifications.
- **OpenAI:** forum text, only while an optional forum AI feature is switched
  on.
- **Sentry:** crash reports. On the website, about 10% of sessions, and every
  session with an error, are recorded, with text masked.
- **Cloudflare:** the website and file storage.
- **Railway:** our servers, in the United States.
- **Resend:** sending email.
- **YouTube and Vimeo:** videos a user embeds in a forum post. When you watch
  one, it loads from that service.
- **Apple and Google:** in-app purchase status from store receipts.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you live in
Canada or Mexico, this means it leaves your country, and it may be accessed
under United States law.

## How long it is kept

- **Account, garden data, saved plants, forum content, push tokens and
  newsletter subscription:** until you remove them or delete your account.
- **Scans:** a scan you have not decided to keep is deleted after 24 hours. A
  scan you keep stays until you remove it or delete your account.
- **Email delivery and bounce records:** 90 days.
- **Email click records:** 90 days.
- **Hashed "do not email" marker:** kept indefinitely.
- **Blog page views:** 90 days.
- **Rate-limiting IP addresses:** briefly, in memory only.
- **Audit log:** 90 days.

### When you delete your account

Deletion is immediate. We email you when it is done.

**What is erased:** your account, profile and avatar; your garden data; your
scans and their photos; your saved plants and your Firebase login; your push
tokens; your newsletter subscription; your block and mute lists; and your
address in our email log. Your forum posts are erased: the text, images and
edit history are removed, and a "[deleted]" placeholder keeps each post's place
so threads still read.

Audit log entries about your account are removed within 90 days by our regular
clean-up.

**What is kept:**

- A record that the account existed and was deleted, with a hashed copy of the
  email address. If staff deleted the account for abuse, or because its owner
  was under 13, we use the hash to block that address from signing up again.
  If you deleted the account yourself, the hash is kept only as the record that
  the account was deleted, and it does not stop you from signing up again.
- The hashed "do not email" marker.
- Moderation records.
- Posts under an open abuse report, until the report is closed.
- Text of yours that other people quoted, and mentions of you, in their posts.
- The other person's copy of your private messages, shown as from "[deleted]".

You can ask to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it. You can delete your account yourself in the app's settings, in
  the website's settings, or at [/delete-account](/delete-account);
- **receive a copy** of it in a portable format;
- **withdraw your consent.**

Email [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to a privacy regulator:

- in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC);
- in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI);
- in Mexico, the INAI, or the authority that succeeds it.
