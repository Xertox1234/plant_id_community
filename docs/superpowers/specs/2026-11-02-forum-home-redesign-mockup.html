Forum home in dark and light (v4)

Same screen in both modes. Light uses Canopy's own light palette: pale green ground, near-white gradient cards, and darker accents (gold, coral, purple, green) so small text and icons keep readable contrast. The logo keeps its dark tile in light mode on purpose: the red cross must never sit on a light background. A's header, Experts, Boards, Who's online and footer, with B's Recent activity. Tap any text-only activity card to expand it (the top one starts open); tap again to close. Picture cards don't expand. Tapping one opens the topic, as now.

What the expanded card shows (my guess)

  • The two latest replies, each with the poster, how long ago, and the first couple of lines.
  • If the topic is solved, the accepted answer instead, tagged "Solution".
  • Who's in the thread (avatars) and who started it.
  • An "Open topic" button. Tapping anywhere else on the card closes it again.

How it behaves

  • Opens in place; the cards below slide down. Nothing else moves.
  • Replies load when you tap, so the screen opens as fast as it does now. Shaped placeholder lines show while they load.
  • With reduced motion turned on, the card opens instantly with no slide.
  • Only one card is open at a time? Not decided yet.

Data

  • The latest replies come from the existing posts endpoint, fetched when the card opens, so no backend change.
  • Who's online is still the one new endpoint.
